Our Mission

Powering India's
EV Revolution

We built Volt路 to make electric vehicle charging as simple and stress-free as possible for every Indian driver.

Why We Built This

Range anxiety is the #1 reason people hesitate to switch to electric vehicles. We believe that with the right tools, every EV driver should feel confident on any journey — whether it's a daily commute or a long highway trip.

Volt路 brings together station discovery, route planning, battery estimation, and facility information into one simple platform — completely free for all users.
